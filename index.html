    The fine print, in plain English

    Holding your date

    A 50% deposit reserves your date. We'll hold a date for 72 hours while you get approval.

    What’s included in the total

    Food, drinks, a 20% service charge and Hawai‘i GET (4.712%). If your total is under the space minimum, the difference is billed as unused minimum. Nothing else is added.

    Changing plans

    Move your deposit to a new date with 14 days’ notice. Final headcount is due 7 days before the event. Guests must be 21+ after 7pm.
